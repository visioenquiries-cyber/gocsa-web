02 · Referral partners — GPs, pharmacies, hospitals, allied health

Support at Home provider Aged care · Adelaide

Information for referrers

General practice · Pharmacies Hospitals · Allied health

GOCSA Community Care is a Support at Home provider based in the Adelaide CBD. We deliver in-home aged care to older South Australians, with particular strength in culturally and linguistically appropriate care for Greek-speaking clients. Every client is assigned a dedicated Care Partner who plans, coordinates and reviews their services, and who is a single point of contact for you as well.

Services

  • Domestic assistance
  • Personal care
  • Nursing care
  • Medication management
  • Continence management
  • Allied health access
  • Meal preparation
  • Transport
  • Social support
  • Home maintenance
  • Aids & equipment
  • End-of-life support

How to refer

  1. Your patient contacts My Aged Care on 1800 200 422, or applies at myagedcare.gov.au, for an assessment.
  2. Once assessed, they can nominate GOCSA as their provider.
  3. Call us on (08) 7088 0500 and we'll guide them through the rest.

What we bring

  • Bilingual staff — English and Greek — and qualified interpreters arranged when needed
  • All staff hold a current police check
  • Care delivered under the Aged Care Quality Standards and the Code of Conduct for Aged Care
  • Assessment in the client's home, usually within 90 minutes, and reviewed at least annually

Good to know

  • Support at Home has 8 funding classifications, plus short-term Assistive Technology & Home Modifications, Restorative Care and End-of-Life pathways
  • Client contributions are determined by Services Australia based on income and assets
  • Office hours 9am–5pm Mon–Fri; no after-hours on-call service
  • Ask us about coverage in your area

Have a patient who needs support at home?

Speak to a Care Partner directly — we're happy to talk through a case before any referral is made.

03 · Families — caring for a parent

Support at Home Aged care · Adelaide

Is Mum or Dad still managing at home?

You don't have to work it out on your own. We help families arrange government-funded care at home — and we'll explain what your parent may be eligible for before you commit to anything.

Signs it might be time to ask

  • The housework is getting away from them
  • Meals are being skipped
  • They've stopped driving
  • A recent fall, or a stay in hospital
  • Medications are hard to keep track of
  • They're seeing fewer people than they used to

How it works

  1. Call us

    Tell us what's happening. We'll talk you through the options — no cost, no obligation.

  2. Get assessed

    My Aged Care arranges an assessment. We can help you make that first call.

  3. Meet your Care Partner

    They visit at home, build the care plan with you both, and stay your point of contact.
