# 00 — Project Charter

_Status: draft for client review · Last updated at Phase 0_

## Purpose

Create one of Australia's highest-quality Community Care digital experiences for
GOCSA — one that communicates trust, compassion, professionalism, community,
heritage, and human connection, and reassures visitors that GOCSA can be trusted
with the care of themselves or someone they love.

## Client & structure

- **Organisation:** Greek Orthodox Community of South Australia (GOCSA), est. 1930, incorporated.
- **Now:** GOCSA Community Care website.
- **Later:** RGHA Retirement Living website — a separate identity and audience,
  but part of one digital ecosystem, inheriting the design system and
  architecture built here.

## What "world-class" means for _this_ audience

The benchmark is exceptional digital product design — but calibrated to the
people who use it. Primary users are older adults, people with disability, and
their family decision-makers, some of whom read Greek more comfortably than
English. For them, quality = **clarity, trust, and effortlessness**, not spectacle.
Where a luxury brand reaches for drama, we reach for calm and legibility.
Accessibility _is_ the premium feel here.

## In scope (Community Care phase)

- Public marketing/information website for GOCSA Community Care services.
- Reusable design system + component library (to be inherited by RGHA).
- CMS so non-developers can maintain content (pending Decision D1).
- Accessibility (target WCAG 2.2 AA), performance, SEO, analytics, and documentation.

## Explicitly out of scope until separately agreed

- RGHA Retirement Living build (foundations only for now).
- Any resident/client portal, bookings, payments, or authenticated area.
- Migration of unrelated GOCSA properties (church, school, festival, etc.).

_These are assumptions. Correct any that are wrong — see `02-discovery.md`._

## Guardrails I will hold, even against instruction

- I will not present invented facts about GOCSA's services, pricing, or
  compliance as real. Aged-care/NDIS content carries regulatory weight;
  placeholder claims here are a liability, not a convenience.
- I will not trade accessibility for visual effect.
- I will not hardcode content that should live in a CMS.
- I will flag, not silently absorb, decisions that are the client's to make.

## Success criteria (to be made measurable in `04-roadmap.md` once discovery lands)

Trust felt on first screen · a family member can find a service and how to start
it within seconds · usable one-handed on a mid-range phone · WCAG 2.2 AA · fast
on a slow connection · editable by GOCSA staff without a developer.
