# 02 — Discovery Questions

_These gate the roadmap and technical strategy. Grouped by how much they unblock.
The four ⭐ questions block the most and are surfaced directly to you first._

## A. Services & purpose (drives information architecture)

- ⭐ **What does GOCSA Community Care actually deliver?** e.g. Home Care Packages,
  Commonwealth Home Support Programme (CHSP), NDIS supports, social/community
  programmes, in-home vs. centre-based, Greek-speaking care. List the real services.
- Who is the primary decision-maker you most want to reach — the older person, or
  the adult child/family member?
- What is the single most important action a visitor should take? (Call? Enquiry
  form? Download? Book an assessment?)
- Do you take referrals from My Aged Care / NDIS, and should the site explain that pathway?

## B. Content & language

- ⭐ **Bilingual scope:** full English/Greek parity across the site, English-primary
  with key Greek pages, or English-only? This is expensive to retrofit.
- Does existing, approved content exist (service descriptions, staff bios, policies)?
- Is there real photography of GOCSA people/places we may use, with consent?

## C. Maintenance & CMS (drives the entire stack)

- ⭐ **After launch, who edits the website?** A non-technical GOCSA staff member, or
  a developer/agency? Be honest — this decides the CMS and the whole architecture.
- How often does content change, and who approves it before it goes live?

## D. Current state & migration

- ⭐ **Is there an existing GOCSA Community Care website?** If so, the URL. We need
  it to preserve SEO, migrate content, and set up redirects.
- Who controls the domain and DNS? Is a domain already chosen?
- Any existing analytics, and any traffic/enquiry data we can learn from?

## E. Compliance, governance, timeline

- Are you a registered aged-care and/or NDIS provider? (Affects required content,
  disclaimers, and complaints/feedback pathways.)
- Privacy: will the site collect personal/health information via forms? (Australian
  Privacy Principles apply.)
- Are there brand guidelines, accessibility policies, or procurement/security
  requirements we must comply with?
- Target launch date, budget envelope, and who signs off at GOCSA?
- Where will this be hosted, and are there IT/security constraints from GOCSA?

## Assumptions we are currently running on (flag any that are wrong)

1. Greenfield build (no existing site to migrate) — _unconfirmed._
2. Marketing/information site only; no login/portal/payments in this phase.
3. Non-technical staff will need to maintain content → a CMS is required.
4. Bilingual (EN/EL) is in scope to some degree.
5. GOCSA is a regulated aged-care/NDIS provider, so content accuracy is a compliance matter.
